04 / Organizations & volunteering

More than just classes and projects.

Some of the things I’ve learned in college didn’t come from a notebook or a classroom. Being involved in organizations and events gave me the chance to work with different people, take responsibility for things outside my usual routine, and learn how to handle situations while they’re happening.

BINUS BVoice

Staff Operator · P3K Coordinator · P3K Mentor
2024 — Present

BVoice has been one of the biggest parts of my college experience so far. I started as a Staff Operator, working behind the scenes during live broadcasts. I manage microphones, audio levels, music, broadcast beds, and the overall broadcast flow while the announcers are on air.

I also work on the audio side outside of live broadcasts, including creating jingles using RadioBOSS, FL Studio, audio mixers, and other tools.

Later, I became the P3K Coordinator and handled five main programs: PPTC, Expo, Training, Seminar, and Training Camp. That role involved coordinating the team, checking preparations, keeping each program on track, and dealing with things that came up along the way.

Now I continue as a P3K Mentor, helping the next coordinator and team through consultations, presentation reviews, planning discussions, and anything else they need during their programs.

KSF

BINUS Kemanggisan Sport-Fest

Public Relations Committee
October 2025

I joined the Public Relations team for BINUS Kemanggisan Sport-Fest, a campus sports event covering five different sports categories.

Each PR member handled communication with different sports PICs, so a lot of my role involved making sure information stayed clear between the committee and participants.

I also helped with registration and participant needs during the event itself.

TFI

Teach For Indonesia

Teaching Volunteer
December 2025

For five sessions, my team and I volunteered at PAUD Umi in West Jakarta and spent time teaching and accompanying the children.

We prepared a different learning theme for each session and took turns leading classroom activities. We also stayed with the children during their breaks and helped with whatever they needed throughout the day.

Teaching young children was very different from what I usually do in college. It taught me to be more patient, explain things in a simpler way, and adjust when things didn’t go exactly as planned.
